# World-state discovery and mapping

World states have no ready-made ticker or complete data table. Signals around a real-world theme are scattered across prediction markets that continually appear, expire, and change. Turning them into a continuous index first requires finding truly relevant markets and understanding how each outcome affects the target state.

That is the role of the **proprietary Signa AI world-state engine**. It does not simply search keywords or average markets in one category. It continuously reads real prediction markets and uses Signa's proprietary discovery and mapping algorithms to organize discrete events into an explainable, computable, and continuously renewable world-state structure.

::: info Signa's core technology layer
Prediction markets form event prices. Signa's proprietary AI discovers valid signals and identifies causality, direction, strength, and information overlap. The index methodology converts those structured results into continuous levels.
:::

## Discovering signal across the market

At each review, Signa AI rediscovers candidates and applies multiple layers:

1. **Market discovery:** identify new propositions that may affect the target state.
2. **Semantic normalization:** parse outcomes, horizons, settlement criteria, and the true meaning of YES/NO.
3. **Causal-chain extraction:** build an event outcome → real mechanism → target state path and remove keyword-only noise.
4. **Direction determination:** decide whether YES strengthens or weakens the state and which outcome belongs in long and short.
5. **Exposure assessment:** combine impact scale, causal strength, outcome differentiation, and data credibility into a structured score.
6. **Deduplication and representation checks:** identify repeated propositions, shared prerequisites, and event families to avoid double counting.
7. **Continuous rolling:** discover replacements as old markets expire so the state remains continuously represented.

Signa Index therefore does more than read prediction-market prices: it reorganizes prediction markets into an index language for real-world trends.

## Why this is not a generic AI summary

A general language model can summarize news but cannot by itself decide whether an event contract qualifies. Signa AI operates under explicit target-state definitions, causal layers, direction rules, quality constraints, deduplication, and version continuity.

AI extracts structured evidence; proprietary algorithms apply consistent scoring and eligibility checks. The same inputs, rules, and versions should yield reviewable structured results. A market that lacks a clear causal path or fails the rules is not included merely because it looks related.

## AI does not perform price discovery

“Discovery” means finding effective markets and structural signals related to a target state. Event prices still come from real prediction-market participants. Signa AI does not invent probabilities from text or directly generate index levels.

Responsibilities are separated:

1. **Prediction markets** form event prices.
2. **Signa AI** discovers candidates and maps causality, direction, and exposure.
3. **Index methodology** converts structure and prices into a continuous series.

## Strong capability requires strong constraints

Free-form AI text is never treated as an index conclusion. Each discovery and mapping becomes a structured record: normalized proposition, target-state definition, long/short judgment, causal explanation, overlap analysis, data and model version, and rule-validation result.

## Rules take priority

AI output is structured analytical input, not a price decision. Explicit rules constrain eligibility, direction consistency, representation, data recency, and version continuity. A new effective index version is formed only after the candidate set passes all checks; if it cannot, the system does not publish a misleading level merely to keep updating.
