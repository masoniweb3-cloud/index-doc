# Launch indexes

The initial Signa Index release covers six real-world themes. Each has independent long and short directions: long expresses a strengthening state; short expresses a weakening state.

The events below illustrate direction only. They are not live components. Current levels, components, and trading status are shown in the Signa product.

| Index | Long typically rises when | Short typically rises when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Middle East Peace Index** | Ceasefires hold, talks advance, hostages are released, normalization progresses, or conflict de-escalates | War persists, military activity escalates, talks fail, or conflict spreads |
| **China National Momentum Index** | Economic resilience, technology capability, international influence, policy stability, or external security improves | Those dimensions materially deteriorate or external pressure rises |
| **United States National Momentum Index** | Economic resilience, technology leadership, institutional operation, policy execution, or international influence improves | Recession risk, institutional conflict, technology leadership, or influence weakens |
| **Europe National Momentum Index** | Economic and monetary resilience, energy security, industrial competitiveness, integration, or influence improves | Economic, energy, industrial, integration, or external-security conditions worsen |
| **AI Development Index** | Model capability, compute, deployment, investment, or a supportive governance environment advances | Progress stalls, compute is constrained, investment contracts, deployment falters, or regulation tightens materially |
| **Cryptocurrency Development Index** | Adoption expands, regulation becomes clearer, institutional participation grows, infrastructure matures, or integration with mainstream finance advances | Adoption contracts, regulation suppresses activity, institutions exit, or a major security and trust crisis occurs |

::: info Reading the examples
An index does not move mechanically because a related news item appears. An event must be an active component, pass direction mapping, and contribute through its market-price change and weight.
:::

## Not YES and NO

Long and short are not the YES and NO outcomes of one binary contract. Each has a continuous level; they do not need to add to 100. Buying short is also not the same as selling long.
