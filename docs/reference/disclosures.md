# Product rules and disclosures

This page describes general boundaries for the documentation, index data, and current index-share product. Eligibility, orders, and funds operations are also subject to the rules shown in the product.

## Information only

These documents explain world-state indexes, six launch themes, trading, and use boundaries. Event examples are directional illustrations, not live components, current levels, or executable quotes.

An index level is not the probability of one event and should not be interpreted as a certain prediction of a real-world outcome.

Every world-state index uses a limited event set and defined methodology boundaries. It cannot capture all information and may be limited by data, market structure, and mapping judgments.

## Not professional advice

Indexes, documentation, and related data are not investment, trading, legal, tax, accounting, or policy advice and do not guarantee results or returns.

## Nature of index shares

Current shares are positions recorded in a Signa account. They are not BEP-20 tokens sent to a wallet, are not freely transferable, and cannot be withdrawn directly. Sell shares back into USDT before withdrawing available USDT.

The index level is not a probability or an inherently USDT-denominated asset price. Shares, amounts, and fees come from the valid product quote and order receipt.

## Custody and availability

After a deposit is confirmed, USDT is custodied by Signa and balances and positions are recorded in the platform ledger. This is not end-to-end self-custody. Multiple checks, separated controls, and ledger records reduce specific risks but do not provide deposit insurance or absolute safety.

Continuous index calculation does not guarantee continuous trading. Data anomalies, expired quotes, insufficient liquidity, blockchain failures, platform controls, or other events may pause or delay deposits, trading, or withdrawals.

## Fees and rule changes

Current public rules include a 1% buy opening fee and 0% sell closing fee. Withdrawal fees, minimums, limits, and confirmation requirements are displayed during the operation and may be updated. Completed orders retain their actual historical records.
