# Risks and suitability

World-state indexes rely on event-contract prices, causal mapping, and dynamic component maintenance. This creates capabilities—and risks—beyond conventional asset indexes.

![Key world-state index risks across event markets, model and mapping, data and settlement, and product-specific factors.](/images/en/10-four-key-risk-categories-en.png)

## Principal risks

### Event-market risk

Contracts may have low liquidity, wide spreads, sensitivity to small trades, or abrupt jumps after breaking events.

### Model and mapping risk

Direction, causal strength, or information overlap can be misjudged. AI assistance cannot eliminate this risk.

### Definition risk

Reality is complex. Any index captures only part of a country, region, or technology and is not a complete evaluation.

### Data and settlement risk

Sources may fail or be corrected; event contracts may be disputed, settled early, or voided.

### Rolling and methodology risk

Dynamic components preserve continuity, but baskets differ across periods. Component and methodology changes can affect historical comparability.

### Product-specific risk

Index shares involve quote availability, tracking difference, liquidity, custody, platform operation, blockchain networks, counterparty, legal, and regional risks. An updating index does not guarantee that buying, selling, or withdrawing is always available.

### Custody risk

After deposit, USDT is custodied by Signa and balances and shares are recorded in its ledger. This is not end-to-end self-custody. Custody services, signing, connectivity, or ledger failures may delay operations or cause loss.

### Exit and quote risk

Historical levels and charts are not executable. Shares can be sold only while the system offers a valid quote and trading is available; market changes, stale prices, liquidity, or safety controls can pause quotes.

## Use boundary

Signa Index does not guarantee event accuracy or represent complete reality. Index data should not be used alone as investment, legal, tax, or policy advice.
