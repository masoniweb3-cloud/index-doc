# Signa Index Documentation VIS

## Design position

The documentation inherits the current frontend's **dark editorial trading system**, while borrowing only the information architecture of the earlier documentation infographics.

The result should feel like the product explaining itself: high-trust, data-dense, calm, and mechanically explicit.

## Four answers

- Narrative role: explanation and auditability, not marketing decoration
- Viewing distance: laptop and tablet documentation reading
- Visual temperature: calm, authoritative, technical, slightly editorial
- Capacity: one mechanism per image, 3–6 stages or one worked example

## Signature system

1. Deep navy canvas instead of a white report background.
2. Warm serif display titles paired with Geist utility text.
3. Emerald represents state, continuity, or positive directional exposure.
4. Amber represents transformation, decision, or product action.
5. Coral is reserved for negative direction, exclusion, and risk.
6. Fine graphite borders and short labels preserve the product's dense trading character.
7. Monospaced numbers make probabilities, weights, and calculations auditable.

## Acceptance criteria

- A reader can state the diagram's takeaway after five seconds.
- All labels are legible at the normal VitePress content width.
- Every number is supplied by the diagram specification.
- No image exists only to occupy space.
- The ten images visibly belong to one system.
